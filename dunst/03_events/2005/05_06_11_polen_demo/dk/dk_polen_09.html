Dear Everyone

In Denmark we also discovered that the embassy was closed saturday. But we were around 20 people all dressed up and did a pride march from the station to the embassy through the most expensive and upper class suburb of Copenhagen, which was quite a show.

In front of the embassy we got to talk to some people walking their dogs and doing garden work. We told them why we were demonstrating and they were all either outraged by the story and wished us the best of luck - or they allready knew about story in Poland and thought it was great what we were doing.
The police only came by once and told us to please occupy a little less of the street. We told them that the cars were passing very slowly anyway.

"Well, people are not used to such colourfull people out here," the nice police woman told us and added: "But they really do need it"

Thansk to everyone who participated out there - hope to get pictures from you soon.
