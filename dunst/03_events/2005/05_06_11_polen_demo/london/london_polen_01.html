Demo at the Ambassy of Polen in London 11. June 2005

Hey heres a brief report on the london soldarity demo with warsaw pride i've also included the 2 postings made to a-infos list from warsaw for people who arent on that list cos the pride went ahead! without permision and they even mention our solidarity actions!

So in london..... about 10 or so people gathered at the polish embassy we had a banner - 'queer rights equal human rights' and people also painted their placards on site the embassy was closed, but people inside paniced and called the police police came and told us we werent supposed to protest in front of the embassy.... we carried on and the police delivered our letter to those inside the embassy.

A couple of the more glamorous protesters wore hot pink drag lots of polish squatter queers showed up we also went and demonstrated outside the institute for polish culture just around the corner.

Big kisses to everyone who got it together to do this action at such short notice.
