HR & FRU dunst 17. 18. & 19. December 2004
Queer d.i.y. mini-festival in the Squat "Ungdomshuset" in Copenhagen, Denmark.

Summary:
HR&FRU dunst (MR&MRS dunst) is a Queer Personality cuntest, where queers from all over the universe are invited to go on stage and show off their personality to the max. Hosted by and arranged in collaboration with activists from Copenhagens oldest squat "www.Ungdomshuset.dk"

In collaboration with Ungdomshuset "dunst" are inviting to a d.i.y. queer bennefit party for queeruption Barcelona and for dunst Copenhagen. It is centered around the event MR&MRS dunst Saturday 18. december, which will be an all nighter with performance, dress up, queer electro bands etc. Read the details below.

Practical Stuff:
Ungdomshuset has a sleeping space for about 30 people. If more queeruptors turn up, we will try to host them privately or to expand the space. Bring sleeping bags etc.
