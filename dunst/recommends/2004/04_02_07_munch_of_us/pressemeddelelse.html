CD Pressrelease - 'munch of us'
Electrical Records present: "Munch of us".
A compilation of house and ambient artists from the electric underground of Copenhagen.

"Munch of us" features a broad selection of house, ambient and electro and contains many contrasts.

The album starts off with hard pumping beats and aggressive lyrics,
then softens up with more pop-like love songs in the middle.
Towards the end you will find ambient tracks like "Moonfluffers" and "Mindtrap" that take you into a floating melancholy dream universe.
Artists featured:
Agent Mr Cool and Little Prince have previously been involved in projects such as Aqua, Outlandish, Infernal, Banjos likørstue, Danser med drenge among others.

Johnny Warehouse feat. The Thom and Hairwerk, making their debut have been well-known for several years on the homosexual scene and have made shows, among other things in connection with "dunst"(an association for homosexuals, bi-sexuals, transvestites etc.)

Kirk Occur consists of three guys from the U.S.A., Norway and Denmark respectively, who have been producing and playing experimental electronic music since 2000.
