Hairwerk:
Hairwerk is a Danish multi artist working with hair, poetry, visual art, performance art, music and installation. 

Hairwerk has had several concerts, performances and exhibitions all over Europe since 1987. In 2005 he was one of the core members of the performance and queer activist group dunst.dk. His most recent work was a solo exhibition of collages, books and a site specific installation at warehouse9.dk in Copenhagen 17 - 23 December 2007 and a solo exhibition 9/2 - 15/3  2008 at the Qbox gallery in Athens.
