Den herboende Færørske sanger Dennis Agerblad fra performancegruppen
dunst demonstrerer med singlen I wanna Pose/Everytime at electro/minimal techno sagtens kan bære en fremtrædende vokal og personlige tekster.

Projektet er et samarbejde med DJ, maler og komponist Minimal Martin, kendt fra den Københavnske klubscene. Sammen har de lavet to perler, hvor Dennis Agerblads new wave vokal smelter sammen med Minimal Martins hypnotiske minimal electro sound.

Dennis Agerblad
Composer, Singer, Songwriter, Performer, Artist, Grafic Designer, Balley Danser, Pilot.

I mostly do music and performance inspired by turning things upside down. Mixing "truth" with "lies". When the audiens don't quite understand what's going on. They cant put the experince in a sertain box. And hopefully an other person had a tolaly different experience. The audiens is allowd to leave a show tolally confused.

This is my way of telling people not to go blind thrue life. But always tjeck out what is right for themself.

I performed is known for experimental and theatrical concerts and has played in Denmark, Faroe Island, Sweden, Germany, France, Switserland, Holland and Spain.

I am composing alone or with Minimal Martin & Mathuresh
