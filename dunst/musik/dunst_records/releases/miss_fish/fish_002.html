Miss Fish
Miss Fish is a performance artist inspired by punk rock, electronic music, puppet theater, dark german silent movies, the beauty of the ocean and the infinity of the mind. 

As a singer and songwriter MISS FISH has collaborated with different musicians & composers including Minimal Martin, Undergram, Mathuresh, Johnny Warehouse, Luis Miguelez. 

Miss Fish is known for experimental and theatrical concerts and has played in Denmark, Germany, England, The Netherlands, Spain and Sweden.
