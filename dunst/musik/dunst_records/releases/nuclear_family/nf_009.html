Nuclear Family
If your favourite pass time is to lie on your pink faux fur bed cover, masturbating with your home made jelly dildo whilst covered in bacon, then there's a good chance that you'll love <3 Nuclear Family.

When Andreas Tange and Nikolaj Lange met in late 2004, neither of them had planned to make a band. But, it was inevitable and after making their stage debut in a squat in Barcelona surrounded by the police during Q8, they went back to Denmark to get married and have their family name Tange Lange.

Since then they've been based in Copenhagen, roaming the queer parts of Europe as often as possible.
They where first released on a compilation by danish queer art collective dunst and later on Crunks Not Dead Records with Scream Club, Heidi Mortensson and others.
