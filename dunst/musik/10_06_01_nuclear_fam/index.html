Nuclear Family - New album 2010
Nuclear Family has made a new album. It's called "FUCK MY HE ART" and it raises the body fluid factor to the max, wether it be messured in blood, sweat, tears, or spunk. The music is also more organic and experimantal than before and mixes traditional synths with, flutes, video games, neo-folk drum-samples, and beats made of breaking glass. But the result is still bouncy electropunk as catchy as ever. You can have a listen and download it in their own webshop for just 5€.
