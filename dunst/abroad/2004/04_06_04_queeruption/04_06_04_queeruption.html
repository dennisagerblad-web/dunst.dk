Event - Queeruption #6 1 - 7 june - 2004
dunst is going to Queeruption to perform friday the 4th and Saturday the 5th of june.
Queeruption 6 will take place from monday june 1st till monday june 7th at the Afrika squat in Amsterdam.

During the week there can be direct actions, political discussions, workshops, shared vegan cooking and eating, experimental queer cinema, transgender porno, concerts and queer cabaret, if *you* make it happen.

This is a Do It Yourself gathering! Do you want to do a workshop, bring your film, perform or have an idea about direct action, make it happen! No fucking yuppie scum welcome!
