The shadow people

The shadow people are everywhere - living in their own little worlds.

They do ordinary things - and live ordinary lives - but they all have extraordinary secrets. They are carefully kept in the shadow - because there is no room for them elsewhere.

The shadow people sing about their deepest dreams, fears and desires: fragile emotions, exhibitionism, erotic fantasies, perversion, androgynous beauty, self hatred, aggression and a deep wish to break out.

In this colourful and visual electro-punk performance we se fragments of what the shadow people are hiding.
