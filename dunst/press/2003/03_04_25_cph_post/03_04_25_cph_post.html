25 April 2003
Dunst is a cultural association for homo and bissexuals in Denmark.

Their last event at Ungdomshuset 6 months ago was a total blast,
a mixture of the regulars at Ungdomshuset (punk-rock anarchists) and Dunst's own network of people who have individual lifestyles,
from transvestites to gay-politics activists.

They will present many acts on stage - last time it got as raw as it gets. This time the event is titled the "Primates Night"
(A primate is the animal category monkeys belongs to - and humans?).
