30 April 2003
Silo Rave

SILO; Islands Brygge 52, Cph Amager; 100kr; 23:00-06:00
Huge party in the wicked rooms of the old corn silo. Four of the best underground clubs in Copenhagen get together to raise money for the Aids fund. The four clubs are Tam Tam, Drama!, Dunst and Lækker who represent the trashy innovative electro-rock side of Copenhagen nightlife.
