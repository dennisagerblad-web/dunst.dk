April 2004
CROSS DRESS! - April the 10th
Once again the spectacular performance art group dunst throws one of their parties. And this time they are back at Undgomshuset (The Youth House) which is a meeting place for punks and the autonomists of the Copenhagen youth. Expect bad behaviour, mind-blowing performances, some of the best DJ's in town, cheap drinks and beers plus freaks and friends in a wonderful mix. Mind though: No hard drugs allowed!

CROSS DRESS!, Ungdomshuset, jagtvej 69,
From 22.00 - ? Entrance 60 kr.
