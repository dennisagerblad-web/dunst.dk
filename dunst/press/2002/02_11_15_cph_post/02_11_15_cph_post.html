15 November 2002
Dunst is a network of Gay artists, it is also a website (www.dunst.dk), and they are currently the people behind the best gay parties in Copenhagen. All takings for the evening will go to support the artistic activities of the Dunst non-profit association.

There will be a sea of extravagant shows throughout the night, from the champion Drag Queens Miss OTB / Hairwerk and Co to the Singing Ass hole and the giant Duck Woman. Jean von Baden and Miss Fish are just two of the many DJs, and Hairwerk will play in the Lesbian lounge.

This over-the-top Gay/Lesbian event is celebrating multi-sexual liberation. It takes place in Ungdomshuset - a super trashy hangout for teenage punk-anarchists and devoted anti-capitalists. Most promising for a great party.
